Clinical Review Assistant - Knowledge Base Seed

Section: Clinical Guideline Hypertension 2.1
For most adults with hypertension, a common blood pressure target is below 130/80 mmHg.

Section: Clinical Guideline Diabetes 1.4
Diabetes diagnosis is supported by fasting plasma glucose >= 126 mg/dL.

Section: Clinical Guideline Diabetes 3.2
A common HbA1c goal for many nonpregnant adults is below 7%.

Section: Clinical Guideline Respiratory 1.2
For stable adults without chronic lung disease, oxygen saturation around 94-98% is generally acceptable.

Section: Clinical Guideline Endocrine 2.3
Thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH) is commonly the first screening test for thyroid dysfunction.

Section: Clinical Guideline Cardiology 4.1
Low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) is a primary target for cardiovascular risk reduction.

Section: Clinical Guideline Cardiology 6.5
After uncomplicated elective coronary stent placement, dual antiplatelet therapy is often continued for about 6 months, based on bleeding and ischemic risk.

Section: Clinical Guideline Pulmonary 2.2
Chest radiograph is a common first-line imaging test for suspected community-acquired pneumonia.

Section: Clinical Guideline Sepsis 1.1
In sepsis workflows, broad-spectrum antibiotics should begin as early as possible, ideally within 1 hour of recognition.

Section: Clinical Guideline Inpatient Care 5.4
Use risk-based venous thromboembolism prophylaxis, pharmacologic or mechanical, based on contraindications and bleeding risk.

Section: Medication Guide Anticoagulation 2.4
Warfarin therapy requires regular INR monitoring and dose adjustment.

Section: Medication Guide Diabetes 1.3
Assess renal function before starting metformin and monitor periodically.

Section: Medication Guide Cardiology 5.4
Do not combine nitrates with phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors due to severe hypotension risk.

Section: Procedure Policy 1.2
Before invasive bedside procedures, discuss indication, risks, benefits, alternatives, and obtain documented informed consent.

Section: Procedure Policy 2.1
Pre-procedure verification includes patient identity, intended procedure, and timeout.

Section: Hospital Policy Medication Administration 1.2
Use at least two patient identifiers before medication administration.

Section: Hospital Policy Privacy 1.1
Patient records are role-restricted and must not be disclosed outside approved channels.

Section: Assistant Behavior Guideline 1.8
